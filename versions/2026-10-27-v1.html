How this is measured

Metric. Prebid revenue per 1,000 pageviews on floored pages (about 80%) vs control pages (about 20%).

Comparison. Each day, a site's gap minus the gap of the 215 sites with no floors change since Oct 15 (same day). The effect is the average of that difference after the change minus before. Only full UTC days count; the day of a change is dropped.

Noise. Each site's normal daily swing is measured on Oct 16–21. t = effect ÷ (daily swing × √(1/days before + 1/days after)). Clear needs 2+ full days after and t of 2 or more.

Sources. Performance: Venatus publisher page (via the shared sheet). Change times: Mile admin audit log.

Terms. Multiplier: scales floor prices (lower = softer floors). Floor stages: three settings in the custom strategy (first, default, AdX); 0 is the lighter setting. Hard floors: bids under the floor are rejected on that browser. seo / usp / aco: names of model training setups.
