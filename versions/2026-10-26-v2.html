How this is measured

Metric. Prebid revenue per 1,000 pageviews on floored pages (about 80%) vs control pages (about 20%). Prebid + AdX adds AdX to both.

Sources. Performance: Venatus publisher sheet. Traffic mix (used only to match similar sites): Mile analytics, Oct 20–26. Settings and change times: Mile admin audit log.

Limits. A single site can swing 3–10 points day to day with no change, so one day is a signal, not proof.

Pending. Tech changed six sites on Oct 27 (bloxd.io, puzzlist.com, framed.wtf, baseballbros.io, pokemonshowdown.com, shellshock.io). Oct 28 is the first full day to judge them.

Terms. Multiplier: scales floor prices (lower = softer floors). Custom floor stages: three settings in the custom strategy (first, default, AdX); 0 is the lighter setting. Hard floors: bids under the floor are rejected on that browser.
