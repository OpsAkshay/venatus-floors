How much revenue is optimised?38% of revenue now sits on sites at or above break-even, up from 20%. 39 sites that were behind before tuning are now ahead (27% of revenue); 49 smaller sites moved the other way (10% of revenue).
How close is break-even?−0.7% across all sites, about $338 a day on roughly $63,000 of daily revenue. A further 44% of revenue sits within 2% of break-even.

How this is measured

Source. Venatus's session floor measurement page: floored sessions (about 80%) vs control sessions (about 20%), bot and ad-blocked pages excluded, site-days with at least 1,000 pageviews in each group.

Gap. For each site and day: floored revenue compared with what the same floored pageviews would have earned at that site's control revenue per pageview that day. Sites and days are then added up, so bigger sites count for more.

Status. At or above break-even: gap of 0% or better. Near: 0% to −2%. Behind: worse than −2%.

Days. Full UTC days, Oct 16–27. Oct 15 (partial first day) and Oct 22 (tuning day) are left out of the before/after windows.
